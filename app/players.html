How these rankings work

Ranks average two free weekly projection sources, RotoWire (through Sleeper) and ESPN, player by player and week by week, then rescore them for the selected profile for the remaining full weeks through Week 17. The current week is excluded because games may already have started. Flex includes RB, WR and TE; quarterbacks, kickers and defenses rank separately. Kickers and defenses use standard K/DST scoring (ESPN’s and Sleeper’s defaults averaged) in every profile, not league custom rules.

This is a projection ranking, not an expert consensus or trade-value chart. General uses standard half-PPR. Camden includes the TE premium and expected exclusive yardage bonuses. Dad uses expected yardage/reception buckets and estimated TD-distance payouts. Connected Sleeper leagues use supported league scoring rules; unknown rules stop publication. Bonus and bucket estimates are a documented approximation, not a validated prediction model. Games counts only weeks with a published projection; missing player weeks are not estimated. Injuries and role changes can affect future projections.

League profiles order Flex by points above estimated replacement, using league size and eligible starter slots. Position lists order by projected points. Recommendations compare same-position bench replacements, protect starters and reserve players, and pause when roster coverage or freshness is uncertain. They do not submit transactions. Kicker and defense suggestions are not made here.

Tiers group players whose ranking values cluster together, found by splitting each list at its largest natural gaps (1-D k-means). Players past the startable range share one last tier. Tier dividers are hidden while searching or filtering ownership.

Schedule rates remaining opponents by the half-PPR points they have allowed to that position in completed weeks, from hardest to easiest fifth among players at the position. It is context only and does not change the rank, because Sleeper’s projections already account for matchups.

Move compares positional rank with the saved snapshot at least five days older; it stays blank until such a snapshot exists. Bye is a remaining bye week, which is different from a week with no published projection.
