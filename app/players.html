How these rankings work

Ranks sum Sleeper’s current half-PPR weekly projections for the remaining full weeks through Week 17. The current week is excluded because games may already have started. Flex includes RB, WR and TE; quarterbacks rank separately.

This is a projection ranking, not an expert consensus or trade-value chart. Standard half-PPR scoring applies, without your league’s TE premium or custom yardage bonuses. Games counts only weeks with a published projection; missing player weeks are not estimated. Injuries and role changes can affect future projections.

Tiers group players whose projected totals cluster together, found by splitting each list at its largest natural gaps (1-D k-means). Players past the startable range share one last tier. Tier dividers are hidden while searching.

Schedule rates remaining opponents by the half-PPR points they have allowed to that position in completed weeks, from hardest to easiest fifth among players at the position. It is context only and does not change the rank, because Sleeper’s projections already account for matchups.

Move compares positional rank with the saved snapshot at least five days older; it stays blank until such a snapshot exists. Bye is a remaining bye week, which is different from a week with no published projection.
